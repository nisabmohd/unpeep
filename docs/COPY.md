# Copy

- Short, plain words. Sentence case everywhere (titles, buttons, labels). No exclamation marks, no "Oops".
- **Never** "safe", "clean", "secure", "protected", "no cameras". Say what was checked:
  "No cameras found on this Wi-Fi", "6 of 7 checks".
- No fear words: "spy", "danger", "threat", "hacker". The listing avoids "spy" too (Play review).
- Results: **Likely camera** · **Worth a look** · **Known**. Always with the reason ("Answers ONVIF and streams on port 554").
- Magnet: "Electronics nearby", "+14 µT above normal". Never "camera detected".
- Lens: "Rings possible lenses. Look closely to be sure."
- Permissions: one line why, asked when the check starts ("To list devices and camera hotspots around you").
- Counts use plurals. Numbers, dates and units follow the locale.
- Every string is in `strings.xml`, translated into de, es, fr, hi, ja, pt-BR. Keep translations as short as English
  where possible; labels must fit 62 dp (the check row) or end in "…".
