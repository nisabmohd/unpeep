# Changelog

Each released version has a `## <version>` section; the release workflow uses it as the release notes.

## 0.1.0
- Wi-Fi check: finds the devices on the Wi-Fi you joined and says which look like cameras, and why.
- Hotspots check: lists the Wi-Fi names around you and flags the ones cheap cameras use for setup.
- Bluetooth check: finds trackers and camera-like names nearby.
- Find it: walk towards a hotspot or Bluetooth device by its signal.
- Lens finder: rings small bright spots that may be lenses, with red and contrast filters, 2× zoom and proof photos.
- Infrared finder: the front camera looks for infrared LEDs, after a TV-remote test.
- Magnet: learns the room's normal and shows when it jumps near electronics.
- Haptics: the same feel for the same meaning on every screen.
- Checklist: the places worth a look by hand, for a stay, a trial room, a washroom, an office or a car, with photos and notes.
- Reports: every sweep ends on a report with what was found, why, what was marked as known (with Undo) and what couldn't be checked; rename, share or export as a PDF.
- If you find a camera: what to do, step by step, and the emergency numbers where you are, offline.
- Welcome, Appearance (theme, colours, font, text size, sweep steps), language, Privacy (delete everything), About, Licences.
- Seven languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese.
